The exact target and prediction contract

Positive = tip_amount ≥ 0.25 × fare_amount, for eligible card trips with finite positive fares and nonnegative recorded tips. Exactly 25% counts. Total amount includes the tip and is forbidden. This target calculator is an illustration of the definition, not a model prediction.

Why a feature was kept or rejected

Groups are added in a fixed greedy order using logistic regression; a gain of at least 0.001 June AP is required. Trees share the selected representation. This order-dependent procedure does not test every possible interaction.

09 / WHAT THE DATA CANNOT TELL US

People are more
than their trip records.

A useful ranking still makes mistakes. The study measures these baselines—not the ultimate predictability of generosity.

♙ ?

Missing human context

No passenger satisfaction, personal preferences or service-quality measurement.

$ ∅

Unobserved cash tips

Cash gratuities are absent. Findings cannot be generalized to cash passengers.

≈ ↔

Uncertainty remains

Similar journeys can end differently. One test month cannot establish year-round performance.

Real failure slices, calibration and statistical limits

These overlapping July slices have at least 200 trips. The 500-resample paired bootstrap treats trips as independent and conditions on the fitted model; it omits selection uncertainty and may understate uncertainty for correlated trips. June was reused for multiple choices. No post-hoc calibration was fitted.
